# James's Portfolio

Language for a small personal portfolio that introduces James and his work to potential teammates and managers.

## Language

**Intended reader**:
A potential coworker or manager considering James as a teammate.

**Personal content**:
James's bio, introduction, and choice of projects, which express his interests and motivations.

**Featured project**:
One of the three deliberately selected projects that lead the portfolio and demonstrate James's abilities, curiosity, and investment in his craft.

**Additional project**:
A selected project available through skill filtering beyond the featured projects. This includes class repositories that provide further evidence of a skill.

**Education**:
A label identifying a project that originated as coursework. It applies equally to featured and additional projects.

**Skill tag**:
A label associating a curated project with a skill it demonstrates. Readers may select several badges to explore projects matching any selected skill.

**Current work**:
A short, personally written account of the motivation and vision behind what James is building or exploring now.
